Data repositories from published work. Code and analyses on OSF.

Repositories

leadershipdata

Cross-cultural leadership data underlying multiple papers on leadership dimensions, evolutionary models, and the Multi-Capital Leadership Theory.

sociallearningdata

Data on teaching and social learning across 23 egalitarian hunter-gatherer societies. Companion to Garfield & Lew-Levy (2025).

violationsandpunishmentsdata

Cross-cultural data on norm violations and punishments across human societies. Companion to Garfield et al. (2023, EHS).

hamarconflictdata

Interpersonal conflict and third-party mediation data from the Hamar of southwest Ethiopia. Companion to Garfield & Glowacki (2023).

reputationdiversitydata

Cross-cultural data on reputation domains across human societies. Companion to Garfield et al. (2021, Phil Trans B).

ehrafpunishmentdata

Cross-cultural punishment data drawn from the eHRAF World Cultures database.

chaburankingsdata

Status and leadership ranking data from the Chabu of southwest Ethiopia.

conflict_resolution

Code and data for cross-cultural analyses of conflict resolution.
